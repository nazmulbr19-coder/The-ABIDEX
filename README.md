🎌 ABIDEX

Your Anime. Your Language. Your Entertainment.

ABIDEX is a free anime streaming application that brings anime and cartoons together in one place. Enjoy your favorite anime in English and Hindi, along with Hindi-dubbed cartoons.

✨ Features

- 🎬 English Anime – 3 available English content providers.
- 🇮🇳 Hindi Anime – 1 Hindi content provider.
- 📺 Hindi Cartoons – 1 dedicated Hindi cartoon provider.
- 🆓 Completely Free – No subscription or payment required.
- 📱 Mobile Support – Enjoy ABIDEX on Android smartphones and tablets.
- 📺 Android TV Support – Watch your favorite anime on your Android TV.
- 💻 Desktop Support – Run ABIDEX on desktop computers using an Android emulator.
